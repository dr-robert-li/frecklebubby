## Newborn (before 4 weeks, up to 3 months): hearing screening

Newborn hearing screening is a test to find babies who have hearing loss. The screening test takes a few minutes and you'll get the results immediately.
Hearing loss is not very common in babies, but finding out as early as possible means you and your baby can get the support you both may need with language and speech.
Newborn hearing screening is usually offered before your baby is 4 weeks old, but it can be done up to 3 months of age.
All babies are offered newborn hearing screening, but it's your choice if you want them to have it.
If you decide not to have the screening test, you'll be given checklists to help you check on your baby's hearing as they grow older. If you have any concerns, speak to your health visitor or GP.
If you're not offered a hearing screening test, ask your health visitor or GP to arrange an appointment, or contact your local hearing screening service.
If the screening test does not give a clear result, it will be repeated or your baby will be offered a second, different test.
