## Nap changes: what the data shows

This is not medical advice and it is not a health guideline. It shows what parents of 700 babies logged in a sleep
tracking app. No health body gives ages for nap changes. Each baby is different.

## 4 naps to 3 naps

In this data, half of the babies changed from 4 naps to 3 naps between 3.4 and 5.5 months.

## 3 naps to 2 naps

In this data, half of the babies changed from 3 naps to 2 naps between 6.2 and 8.5 months.

## 2 naps to 1 nap

In this data, half of the babies changed from 2 naps to 1 nap between 10.7 and 14.7 months.

## How long a nap change takes

A change is not sudden. In this data, a change took about 3 to 10 weeks for half of the babies. In that time the number of naps is
different from day to day.

## Limits of this data

Parents do not log every nap. A nap that is not logged looks like a dropped nap, so these ages can be too early. The babies are from families that use a tracking app, mostly in the
United States.
