## Signs of bleeding from the bottom

You might be bleeding from the bottom if you have:
- blood on your toilet paper
- red streaks on the outside of your poo
- blood in your poo or bloody diarrhoea
- very dark poo (this can be blood mixed in poo)

## When to get help

See a GP if your child has blood in their poo.
Ask for an urgent GP appointment or get help from NHS 111 if your poo is black or dark red, or you have bloody diarrhoea.
Go to A&E or call 999 if you're bleeding non-stop, or there's a lot of blood.
