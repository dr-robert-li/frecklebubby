A baby tracker app you can own

Log the day, see the patterns, and ask an assistant that tells you where each answer comes from.

Join the waitlist See the code

Frecklebubby is open source. You can also host it on your own server.

1

Log the day in one tap

Sleep, feeds, nappies, growth, medicine, activities and more. Timers keep running when you close the app.

  • Or write plain words: "120 ml formula 20 minutes ago".
  • More than one child, and more than one carer.
2

See the patterns

The week and the month show each day as one row of 24 hours. Sleep is a bar. Feeds and nappies are marks.

  • Reports with the average and the common range.
  • Growth on WHO percentile curves.
3

Personalize your child's sleep plan

The plan starts from the last 14 days of your child, not from a fixed table. When the logs are outside a recommended range, the plan moves toward the range in small steps and tells you what it changed.

4

An assistant that shows its sources

Ask a question, and the answer has citations to medical sources such as the NHS, the CDC and the WHO. You can open each source.

General information, not medical advice.

5

Voice commands, with hands free AI voice control

Say "Hey Bubby, she had a wet nappy", and the app logs it. Use the talk button, or hands free with the keyword. The app can read the answer aloud.

Frecklebubby is in a closed alpha

  • Only invited people can sign in. There is no public sign-up.
  • The app can change between versions, and it can have faults.
  • The app gives general information. It is not medical advice, and it does not replace your doctor or nurse.
