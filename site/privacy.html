Privacy policy

Last change: 23 November 2026

Frecklebubby is a baby tracker app. This policy tells you what data we keep, where it is, who can get it and how to delete it.

1. Who we are

The operator is Robert Li, a person in Queensland, Australia ("we"). Contact: privacy@frecklebubby.app.

This policy applies to:

  • the website frecklebubby.app, and
  • the hosted app my.frecklebubby.app, which is a closed alpha for invited people.

Frecklebubby is open source, and other people can run their own copy. This policy does not apply to those copies. The person who runs a copy is responsible for its data.

3. Data we do not keep

  • Voice commands. The recording goes to our server, which changes it into text. The recording is then discarded.
  • Passwords. We never get the password of your sign-in account. If you import data from another app, its password is used only during the import and is not stored.
  • Advertising and analytics data. We use no advertising, no analytics service and no tracking cookies.

If one of these points changes, we will change this policy and tell each family owner before the change.

3a. Cry recordings

The cry helper records up to 10 seconds of sound and sends it to our server, which analyses it.

  • If you do not save a crying entry, the recording is discarded. It is never written to a disk.
  • If you save the crying entry from the cry helper, we keep the recording with these labels: the reason that you selected, what helped, the guess of the app, the age of your child in days, and the time since the last feed and the last sleep. The labels have no name and no date of birth.

Purpose: to train a better sound model for the cry helper.

Who can get it: the operator. The recordings and labels are also added to a restricted public dataset. It is open only to researchers, who must ask for permission, and the operator gives or refuses it.

For how long: until you delete the crying entry, the child or the family. Then the recording is deleted from our server, and from the dataset at its next update.

How to turn it off: the family owner clears the checkbox in Settings > Options of this server. It is on by default. After that, no new recording is kept. The recordings from before stay, until you delete their entries.

The voice of a child and "what helped" can identify a person. Do not save the entry if other people speak in the recording and you do not want that kept.

The family owner gets a message about this one time in the app.

4. Sign-in, and data from Google

Sign-in is done by Clerk (Clerk, Inc., United States). You can sign in with Google, with GitHub, or with an email address and a password. Clerk keeps your sign-in account and sets the cookies that are necessary to keep you signed in.

When you sign in with Google, Clerk gets your name, your email address and your profile picture from Google. Our server gets only your name and your email address. We use this data only to sign you in and to connect you to your family. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and we give it to no other party than the ones in section 6. We do not get access to your Gmail, your contacts, your calendar or your files.

5. The assistant and the AI models

The language model, the speech model and the cry sound model all run on our own server. Your logs, your questions and your recordings are not sent to an AI company. Section 3a tells you what we do with cry recordings.

There is one exception, and you control it. If you connect an external AI agent (for example Claude) to your account, that agent can read and change the logs of your family. The company of that agent then gets the data that the agent reads. You can remove the connection at any time.

7. Where the data is, and for how long

The data of the app is on one server in Queensland, Australia, which the operator controls. The connection to it is encrypted (HTTPS).

We keep the data until you delete it:

  • You can change or delete each entry in the app.
  • The family owner can delete the family, with all its children and logs, in Settings > Delete data.
  • You can delete your account in the same place.
  • Your sign-in account at Clerk is separate. Send us an email and we delete it.
  • Send us an email and we delete your waitlist answers.

If the hosted service stops, we will tell you before and give you time to export your data. Then we delete it.

8. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also export the logs of each child as a CSV file in Reports. Write to privacy@frecklebubby.app. We answer in 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) or to the data protection authority of your country.

9. Children

The app is for parents and carers who are 18 years or older. A parent or guardian enters the data of a child. Children do not use the app.

10. Security

Access is by invitation, each request needs a sign-in, and each family can see only its own data. The service is an alpha version. No system is fully safe, and we cannot promise that a fault will never occur. If a fault exposes your data, we will tell you.

11. Changes to this policy

We put the date of the last change at the top. If a change is important, we tell each family owner in the app or by email before it applies.
